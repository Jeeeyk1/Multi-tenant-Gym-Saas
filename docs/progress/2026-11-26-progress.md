## Badge rarity effects (branch `feat/badge-rarity`, stacked on `refactor/shared-authz`)

Owner's idea: rare badges should look special, "like flaming". Thresholds agreed and can be tuned later.

### Rule (API, `modules/badges/domain/badge-rarity.ts`)
- **Legendary:** held by 5% or less of active members, in a gym with 20+ active members.
- **Epic:** 15% or less, in a gym with 10+ active members.
- Smaller gyms get no rare badges; otherwise everything would look rare.

### Built
- **API:**
  - `BadgeRarityService` turns the cached holder counts (15 min) into `{ sharePct, rarity }` by badge key.
  - `rarity` is added to the collection, the equipped list (which also gains `key`, as in the collection) and a
    member's own badge list.
  - `BadgeRarity` enum; tests for the rule and for rarity on equipped badges. 483 tests pass.
- **Mobile:**
  - `BadgeMedal` takes `rarity`. `RarityAura` draws a pulsing warm glow plus five flickering flames for
    Legendary, and a pulsing violet glow for Epic.
  - Medals under 40pt (chat, leaderboard, Who's Here) and reduce-motion users get a still glow only, so long
    lists don't run dozens of animations.
  - Labels: "NEW LEGENDARY BADGE" in the celebration, and "Legendary · 3% of members" on the detail card.
- **Web:**
  - The same effect with CSS keyframes (still under 36px, and off for `prefers-reduced-motion`).
  - The rarity label shows in the popover and on the member page.
  - `BadgeRarity` is added to `@gym-saas/contracts`.

### Dev data (`db/seeds/005_dev_fixtures_members.sql`, dev-only by name)
- 24 extra DEVGYM members, `member01…24@devgym.com` / `Password123!`, with seeded badges. DEVGYM now has 25
  active members:
  - First Step and First Month are common;
  - On Fire is **Epic** (3 holders, equipped by member01–03);
  - Century Club is **Legendary** (member04, equipped);
  - Unstoppable is **Legendary** and belongs to the dev member, unseen, so the "NEW LEGENDARY BADGE" celebration
    shows. It's equipped only if their Consistency slot is free.
  - Three chat messages from test members show badges in chat.
- Idempotent (applied twice locally). Also applied to the local DB.
- The rarity count is cached: up to 1 hour for entries left by the old code, 15 minutes after that. Restart
  Redis to see it at once.
